    Frequently asked questions

    We love to hear from you. If you have a new question, email info@seesaywrite.com

    Do you teach live classes?
    The academy includes pre-recorded, clear, step-by-step lessons only. When your child follows the steps, it is like having a 1 to 1 tutor.
    I'm a homeschooler, will this program work for my child?
    Our best reviews are from parents that homeschool their kids. That's because they follow the program consistently and watch my advice videos. If you're a homeschooler, I'm here for you!
    I am a teacher, can I use this program in my classroom?
    Many schools are using it as a low-cost phonics program. It requires no planning for teachers. Some schools use it for interventions. If you follow the program, your students will learn to read or we will give your money back.
    I am an adult that wants to learn English. Is this Academy for me?
    The content is made for kids. It is also aimed at beginner readers. However, several adults have used our content to learn to read English. Many parents who speak English as an additional language have improved their reading skills by watching the lessons with their kids. I recommend using the free trial to see if it is for you.
    My child is not aged 4-7, is the content still useful?
    If your child is aged 8-10 and they cannot yet read and write in English, this Academy is for them. If your child is dyslexic, please read my other FAQ. Children aged 10+ may find the content is too young for them. Children aged 3 have benefited from our academy, but this is a little younger than national averages.
    How long does it take?
    We recommend your child completes one short lesson every day at the same time (kids love routine and consistency). If you follow this advice, a complete beginner reader will complete the whole academy within 3–4 months. However, every child is different and it's normal to return to some old lessons for revision and practice. Younger children (aged 4) will need more revision than older children (aged 6).
    How much does it cost and can I cancel?
    The first month is free, then after that it is $11.99 per month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time. It is very easy, and if you have any problems, we will help you cancel straight away.
    My child is dyslexic, will this program help them?
    It is very likely to help, however the best strategies for dyslexic children are tailored and personalised. Studies show that early intervention and additional support is crucial for dyslexic children. The earlier the support, the more impactful it is. Meta-studies show that early phonemic awareness and phonics instruction often helps dyslexic learners overcome their language processing issues, especially those that aren't severely affected. Our Academy gives crucial phonics and phonemic awareness instruction.
    Does this Academy teach phonics?
    Yes, our method teaches phonics directly. Each new sound is introduced one at a time, alongside blending skills. Knowledge is built sequentially. The latest research advises that children are taught using a mix of phonics and sight words (the best of both worlds).
